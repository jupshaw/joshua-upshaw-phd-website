BACKGROUND

Who I Am

I am a PhD-trained cognitive neuroscientist, applied clinical data scientist, and healthcare AI founder. My professional work connects rigorous experimental methods with the practical challenge of developing technologies that improve people's lives.

At the University of Arkansas, I studied attention, cognitive control, and emotional processing through EEG, event-related potentials, eye tracking, and behavioral experiments. At Hidalga Technologies, I applied the same analytical discipline to real-world oncology data, clinical machine learning, and healthcare workflow product development.

I am motivated by consequential research questions, strong interdisciplinary teams, and the opportunity to translate scientifically grounded findings into useful tools. My interests extend to neurotechnology, cognitive assessment, wearable biosensing, healthcare AI, and responsible, human-centered innovation.
