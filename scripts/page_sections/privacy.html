WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy and analytics

This personal professional portfolio is operated by Joshua Upshaw, PhD. It provides research, career, and contact information. Please avoid sharing patient records, protected health information, or other confidential information through the website.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics 4 may be used to understand aggregate website traffic and engagement, such as which portfolio pages visitors use. Google Tag Manager is loaded only when a visitor opts in to analytics. Its container must be configured to run only appropriate measurement tags and respect consent. If you decline, the Google Analytics tag does not load. You can revise your choice using the “Analytics preferences” control in the footer.

The website does not use Google Ads remarketing or advertising personalization through this analytics integration. Google's processing and retention practices are described in its privacy policy.

Contact and virtual meeting requests

When secure online forms are enabled, information you voluntarily submit, such as your name, email, organization, message, or meeting request, is processed to respond to your inquiry. The submission service uses a Cloudflare Worker, Cloudflare Turnstile to deter automated abuse, and an email delivery service. The website does not display submitted inquiries publicly.

Cloudflare and the email delivery provider may process information as necessary to provide the submission service. Meeting requests do not create automatic calendar reservations.

Data minimization

Website analytics do not intentionally include contact-form field contents, email addresses, or discussion topics in analytics events. Operational security systems may still process request metadata such as IP addresses in accordance with their service terms. The site does not intentionally collect medical information.
