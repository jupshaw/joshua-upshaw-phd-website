PREREGISTERED · 2025

Influence of Smartphone Notifications on Behavior and Eye Movement Parameters of Emotional Attention

Research question. How do emotional facial expressions and smartphone alerts influence visual attentional orientation, and how are those effects moderated by individual differences?

Study design. A preregistered 360-trial dot-probe experiment with 50 enrolled participants, emotional facial stimuli, auditory notification conditions, and eye movement and pupil measurements.

Findings and implications. The dissertation reported context-dependent differences in gaze orientation, pupillary activity, and performance associated with emotional faces, auditory alerts, and individual characteristics. These findings inform questions for experimental attention research.
