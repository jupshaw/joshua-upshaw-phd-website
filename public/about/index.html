ABOUT

Science with human purpose.

I'm Joshua Upshaw, PhD, a cognitive neuroscientist and healthcare technology founder who connects rigorous research, technical execution, and purposeful leadership.

MY APPROACH

From fundamental questions to meaningful technology

My scientific training centers on attention, cognitive control, emotion, and the ways technology shapes human behavior. I use experimental research, EEG, eye tracking, and statistical analysis to study difficult questions with careful measurement.

At Hidalga Technologies, I expanded that foundation into oncology clinical data science and healthcare AI product development. As founder, CEO, and principal investigator, I set research direction, developed machine-learning analyses, translated stakeholder findings into product requirements, and led interdisciplinary development work.

I value work that combines methodological rigor with practical judgment: define the problem, understand the people involved, test assumptions, and design solutions that make a useful difference.

My professional interests span healthcare AI, clinical analytics, neurotechnology, physiological monitoring, and research-driven product leadership.
